About This Site

Your comprehensive guide to Drama, Debate & Forensics

What is this place?

DDF Hub is a student-created resource designed to help new competitors learn about individual Drama, Debate, and Forensics events. Whether you're looking to get started in your first year or trying to find more in-depth guides to events, this place is for you. Featuring not only resources used by achieving DDFers, but also guides written by DDFers, this is your one-stop-shop for everything DDF.

Our Mission

We believe that Drama, Debate, and Forensics is a crucial activity that helps foster critical thinking and public speaking within everyone that participates. It also provides a strong sense of community, and you'll make friends that will last.

What You'll Find Here

On DDF Hub, you'll discover:

  • Event Guides: Comprehensive guides to every event, written by someone who has gone to state with that event.
  • Quick Reference: Cheat sheets for quick access and information on an event
  • Contact & Support: Get in touch if you have questions or suggestions
