About DDF Hub

Your comprehensive guide to Drama, Debate & Forensics

What is DDF Hub?

DDF Hub is a student-created resource designed to help new competitors and enthusiasts learn about Drama, Debate, and Forensics events. Whether you're looking to get started in your first year or seeking to deepen your understanding of these competitive activities, DDF Hub provides clear, accessible information to guide your journey.

Our Mission

We believe that Drama, Debate, and Forensics are transformative activities that develop critical thinking, public speaking, and creative skills. Our mission is to make these opportunities more accessible by providing a centralized, easy-to-navigate resource for students, coaches, and parents.

What You'll Find Here

On DDF Hub, you'll discover:

  • Event Guides: Detailed information on each DDF event, including time limits, team sizes, and rules
  • Category Overviews: Learn about Debate, Drama, and Forensics categories
  • Quick Reference: Cheat sheets and comparison tables for quick access to competition details
  • Contact & Support: Get in touch if you have questions or suggestions

About the Creator

DDF Hub was created by Noah Coleman, a dedicated member of the DDF community who wanted to make it easier for new students to get involved in these amazing activities. If you have suggestions for improvements or would like to contribute, feel free to reach out!
