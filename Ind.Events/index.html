Events

A concise cheat-sheet listing of official ASAA DDF (Drama, Debate & Forensics) events and short descriptions.

Debate Events

Lincoln-Douglas Debate

One-on-one debate centered on values and philosophical propositions using the season topic.

Policy Debate

Two-person teams argue for or against a policy resolution, emphasizing evidence, research, and plan implementation.

Public Forum Debate

Two-person teams debate a timely public-policy topic with emphasis on clear argumentation and audience persuasion.

Drama / Acting Events

Duet Acting

Two performers present a memorized scene from a published play emphasizing characterization and interaction.

Improvised Duet Acting

Two performers are given a prompt and limited prep time to present an improvised scene, highlighting spontaneity and teamwork.

Solo Acting

A memorized monologue or scene performed by a single actor focusing on character and dramatic truth.

Pantomime

A silent, physical performance without props that tells a story through movement and gesture.

Reader's Theatre

A group presentation where performers read and interpret a script emphasizing vocal expression rather than full staging.

Forensics / Speech Events

Dramatic Interpretation

A memorized solo performance of dramatic material from published sources focusing on emotional and textual interpretation.

Humorous Interpretation

A memorized solo performance using comedic material and timing drawn from published works.

Duo Interpretation

Two performers present a memorized piece from a published work without directly interacting with each other for staging purposes.

Extemporaneous Commentary

A short, conversational-style speech on a current-affairs topic prepared with limited time and minimal notes.

Extemporaneous Speaking — Domestic (U.S.)

Prepared, limited-preparation speeches on U.S. current events and public affairs.

Extemporaneous Speaking — International (Foreign)

Prepared, limited-preparation speeches on international current events and foreign affairs.

Informative Speaking

A researched, memorized speech designed to inform an audience about a topic; visual aids may be optional per tournament rules.

Original Oration

An original, memorized persuasive or inspirational speech written and delivered by the student.

Source: ASAA DDF Handbook
