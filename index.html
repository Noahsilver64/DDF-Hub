What is DDF?

DDF, (Drama, Debate, Forensics) is an activity in which we perform. What we perform varies, ranging anywhere from an informative speech to a completely improvised seven minute play. At its core, DDF is an activity about improving your confidence. It's an activity that will help you land a job, ace a college interview, even just talk to people with more confidence!
